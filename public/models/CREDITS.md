# Model credits

- `bearded-man.stl`: "Portrait of a Man" (marble, about 150 BC). Scan by Oliver Laric, threedscans.com
  (source: https://threedscans.com/uncategorized/portrait-of-a-man/). The site states its scans can be
  downloaded and used without copyright restrictions. Decimated from 998,996 to 79,998 triangles and
  re-oriented by `scripts/decimate-stl.mjs`. The original download is kept locally in `inputs/` (not in Git).
