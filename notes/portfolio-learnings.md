## Patterns across the portfolio
- **Most videos end on a Phillips Digital logo.** Always trim endings when cutting a reel, and keep the one true logo moment for the end.
- **Duplicates by template:** lost-data / red-glitch ("data restored"), flat2d v4 / v11 / popzoom. Use one of each in a reel.
- **Mixed formats:** 24 and 29.97 fps (conform everything to 30), 4:5 (Laser-Grid) and 9:16 (gold-case), 1440p and 720p. Plan reframes before cutting.
- **Signature palette:** lime + black/steel (samurai, mutant, pixel-sneaker, preview), hot pink + cobalt (dance, grillz, flat2d), red (statue, red-glitch, ruby). Group shots by palette for smooth runs; cut across palettes on the beat for impact.
- **Third-party audio** (popout-lyric) must be muted. Source audio from the other videos is synthesised or library sound; a reel needs its own soundtrack.
- **Darkest/slowest** (statue, shoe-slime, samurai opening, preview static) suit a suspense opening; **fastest** (graffiti-flip, kinetic-symbols, kingz, red-glitch, dance) suit the peak.

## To work faster next time
- Survey first: `tools/ai-venv/bin/python scripts/showreel/survey.py notes/frames/<job> <videos...>` (about 2 min for 28 videos), then read the sheets and this table.
- Drive videos over 10 MB: direct download `https://drive.usercontent.google.com/download?id=<id>&export=download&confirm=t` works for link-shared files; a "Sign-in" HTML page means the file is not shared by link (ask the user).
