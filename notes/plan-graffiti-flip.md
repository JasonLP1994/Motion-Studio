# Graffiti Flip → Phillips Digital (16:9, 8 s)

Picks: Words = PHILLIPS DIGITAL + "Phillips" tag · Format = 16:9, ~8 s · Visuals = graffiti fonts + hand-drawn tags (photocopy textures added: core to the style, free in code) · Polish = strobe poster flip, tag sprays on, gritty beat + spray SFX.
Reference: `references/grunge-ref.mp4` (Pinterest screen recording). Measured at full frame rate: a loop of 10 posters, each held exactly 0.133 s (4 frames at 30 fps = one 16th at 112.5 BPM), hard flips, nothing moves inside a poster; the 10-poster cycle repeats every 1.33 s. Every poster: the same bold grotesk headline, one hand-drawn tag or doodle, B&W photocopy textures (concrete, chain-link fence, flat black) or hot pink, tiny corner text, ® marks. Grammar only: no copied words, tags, doodles or images.
